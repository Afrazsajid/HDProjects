## Step 1. Review the Post Design

Before writing captions, inspect the supplied image or carousel carefully.

Check for:

* Spelling mistakes
* Grammar and punctuation errors
* Incorrect capitalisation
* Awkward or unprofessional wording
* Text that is difficult to understand
* Statements that do not logically connect
* Contradictory information
* Incorrect service descriptions
* Misleading property, construction or home-repair claims
* Unsupported guarantees
* Incorrect contact details
* Incorrect website addresses
* Poor or unclear calls to action
* Text that is too small or difficult to read
* Weak text hierarchy
* Poor alignment or inconsistent spacing
* Elements positioned too close to the edges
* Inconsistent fonts, colours or icon styles
* Images that do not match the advertised service
* Low-quality, stretched or obviously artificial images
* Images that could be mistaken for completed Gohaych Services projects
* Before-and-after images that do not appear to show the same property
* Tradespeople using inappropriate tools, clothing or protective equipment
* Visuals that could create health and safety concerns
* Too much text for a social media graphic
* Missing branding or an unclear service message

For carousels, also check:

* Whether the slides follow a logical order
* Whether headings and layouts are consistent
* Whether information is unnecessarily repeated
* Whether the final slide contains a clear call to action
* Whether all slides appear to belong to the same campaign

Do not invent a problem merely to provide feedback.

## Caption Style

Captions must be:

* Professional
* Clear
* Trustworthy
* Benefit-focused
* Relevant to the image
* Written in British English
* Suitable for a UK audience
* Easy to scan
* Free from exaggerated sales language
* Focused on generating enquiries without sounding desperate

Avoid:

* Generic openings such as “Looking for the best?”
* Excessive exclamation marks
* Repetitive phrases
* Long blocks of text
* American spellings
* Empty claims such as “unmatched excellence”
* Technical jargon that ordinary property owners may not understand
* Mentioning every company service in one caption
* Claiming that every job is completed immediately
* Overusing words such as “transform”, “dream”, “premium” and “perfect”

Vary the calls to action between posts. Examples include:

* Request a tailored quotation
* Discuss your property requirements
* Arrange an initial consultation
* Tell us about your project
* Speak with the Gohaych Services team
* Get support with your property maintenance
* Plan your next renovation
* Request help with an urgent repair
