## About the Company

Compare Home Upgrades is a UK comparison and quotation service. We:

* Help homeowners receive and compare quotations from different independent installers.
* Help customers find a suitable installer for their home improvement project.
* Do not personally carry out installations.
* Do not claim that installers are employed, owned, approved, certified or guaranteed by us unless this is explicitly confirmed.
* Do not promise the cheapest price, guaranteed savings, guaranteed quality or guaranteed results.

## Step 2: Write Platform-Specific Captions

Create separate captions for Facebook and Instagram in natural **British English**.

### Facebook Caption

* Write a clear, informative and trustworthy caption.
* Use short paragraphs that are easy to read.
* Explain that customers can compare quotations from independent installers.
* Focus on the homeowner’s problem and the benefit of comparing options.
* Include a relevant call to action.
* Do not make unsupported promises.
* Include 3 to 5 relevant hashtags.

### Instagram Caption

* Use a stronger opening hook.
* Keep the caption engaging and easy to scan.
* Use suitable emojis, but do not overuse them.
* Explain that Compare Home Upgrades helps customers compare quotes and connect with suitable independent installers.
* Include a clear call to action.
* Include 8 to 12 relevant UK-focused hashtags.

## Writing Rules

* Use UK spelling, grammar and terminology.
* Match the caption to the exact service shown in the image.
* Do not describe Compare Home Upgrades as an installation company.
* Do not say “our installers” unless the wording makes it clear that they are independent installers.
* Prefer phrases such as:

  * “Compare quotations from independent installers”
  * “Find a suitable installer for your project”
  * “Explore quotations from different installers”
  * “We help connect homeowners with independent installers”
* Avoid repetitive, robotic or overly sales-focused wording.
* Do not use misleading urgency or guarantees.
* Do not invent prices, discounts, accreditations, locations, contact information or service details.
* Only mention information that is visible in the image or provided by me.
