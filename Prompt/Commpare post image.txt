Act as a senior UK social media strategist, content planner, home-improvement marketing specialist and brand compliance reviewer for **Compare Home Upgrades**.

Create **30 unique, targeted and practical social media post ideas** for Facebook and Instagram.

## About Compare Home Upgrades

Compare Home Upgrades is a UK quotation and comparison service for homeowners.

The company:

* Helps homeowners receive and compare quotations from different independent installers.
* Helps customers find a suitable independent installer for their project.
* Does not personally complete installations.
* Does not employ, own or directly manage the installers unless specifically confirmed.
* Does not guarantee the cheapest price, guaranteed savings, guaranteed quality or guaranteed results.
* Must not present itself as a construction, installation or home-repair company.

## Main Objective

Create content ideas that:

* Generate homeowner enquiries.
* Encourage people to request and compare quotations.
* Educate homeowners before they choose an installer.
* Build trust in the quotation-comparison process.
* Increase website visits and form submissions.
* Encourage homeowners to make informed decisions.
* Position Compare Home Upgrades as a helpful comparison service.
* Remain fully consistent with the company’s actual business model.

## Target Audience

The posts should target UK homeowners who are:

* Planning home improvements.
* Comparing project costs.
* Unsure which installer to choose.
* Looking for quotations from different independent installers.
* Renovating, repairing or upgrading their property.
* Concerned about choosing an unsuitable quotation.
* Trying to understand prices, timelines, materials and project options.

## Business and Compliance Rules

Never suggest that Compare Home Upgrades personally carries out the work.

Do not use phrases such as:

* “Our builders”
* “Our installers”
* “We install”
* “We repair”
* “We renovate your home”
* “Guaranteed cheapest quote”
* “Best installer guaranteed”
* “Approved installers”
* “Fully vetted installers”
* “Guaranteed savings”
* “Guaranteed results”

Only use “approved”, “verified”, “vetted”, “certified” or similar wording if I explicitly confirm that the claim is accurate.

Use compliant wording such as:

* “Compare quotations from independent installers.”
* “Explore options from different installers.”
* “Find a suitable installer for your project.”
* “Request quotations for your home-improvement project.”
* “We help homeowners connect with independent installers.”
* “Compare the details before making your decision.”
* “Review quotations based on price, scope, materials and timescale.”

Do not invent:

* Prices
* Discounts
* Savings percentages
* Installer qualifications
* Accreditations
* Guarantees
* Customer statistics
* Review scores
* Project completion times
* Service areas
* Contact information

## On-Image Text Rules

Suggested on-image text must:

* Be short and easy to understand.
* Use natural UK English.
* Normally contain no more than 10 words.
* Avoid misleading promises.
* Match the purpose of the post.
* Be suitable for a professional social media design.

## Visual Direction Rules

Every visual suggestion should explain:

* The property or project shown.
* Whether people should appear.
* The camera angle or composition.
* The recommended focal point.
* Suitable text placement.
* Whether the design should be photographic, infographic-based or illustration-based.
* How the image should communicate quotation comparison rather than direct installation.

The visual must not suggest that Compare Home Upgrades owns the installer or personally carries out the project.
