## Step 1. Analyse the Post

Before writing captions, examine the supplied content for:

* Spelling mistakes
* Grammar and punctuation errors
* Incorrect British English
* Illogical or confusing statements
* Conflicting information
* Incorrect job titles
* Misleading claims
* Poorly written calls to action
* Unclear messaging
* Text that is difficult to read
* Incorrect contact details
* Information that does not suit 1st Workforce
* Restricted words listed above
* Claims that cannot be verified from the post or brand information
* Layout or design problems that may affect readability

### Issue Classification

If there is a serious issue that could mislead viewers, damage the brand or publish incorrect information, begin with:

🔴 **Important Issue**

Clearly quote the incorrect text and provide the corrected version.

If the issue is minor, do not use a red symbol. Begin with:

**Minor correction:**
Briefly explain the issue and provide the corrected wording.

If no problems are found, write:

**Post review: No grammatical, logical or factual issues identified.**

Do not invent an issue merely to provide feedback.

## Step 2. Identify the Audience

Determine whether the post is aimed at:

1. Businesses looking for recruitment or staffing support
2. Jobseekers looking for opportunities
3. Both businesses and jobseekers

Adapt the message, tone and call to action accordingly.

Do not write “we are hiring” unless the post clearly promotes a genuine vacancy.

## Step 3. Write Platform-Specific Captions

Create separate captions for:

### Instagram

* Engaging and easy to scan
* Use a strong opening hook
* Use short paragraphs
* Add suitable emojis naturally
* Include the telephone number when relevant
* Include 8 to 15 relevant hashtags
* Do not place a clickable website link in the main caption unless specifically requested
* Use a CTA such as “Call us”, “Send us a message”, “Apply today” or “Speak with our recruitment team”

### Facebook

* Friendly, clear and informative
* Provide slightly more detail than Instagram
* Include the telephone number and relevant website link
* Encourage enquiries, applications or direct messages
* Include 4 to 8 relevant hashtags
* Avoid exaggerated or pushy sales language

### LinkedIn

* Professional, credible and B2B-focused
* Focus on recruitment challenges, operational support, candidate quality or career opportunities
* Use complete paragraphs with minimal emojis
* Include a professional call to action
* Include the relevant website link
* Add 3 to 6 industry-specific hashtags
* Do not make unsupported claims about results, experience, placements or client satisfaction

## Caption Requirements

All captions must:

* Use natural British English
* Match the message shown in the post
* Be written specifically for each platform
* Sound professional but approachable
* Avoid repeating the exact wording shown on the graphic
* Avoid generic and robotic introductions
* Avoid excessive emojis
* Avoid keyword stuffing
* Avoid American spelling
* Avoid restricted terms
* Clearly explain what 1st Workforce provides
* Include a relevant and direct call to action
* Use only information supported by the post or the supplied company information
* Avoid promising guaranteed jobs, guaranteed placements or guaranteed candidates
* Avoid saying every candidate is fully vetted unless that information is confirmed
* Avoid “perfect candidate”, “best staff” or similar absolute claims
* Never imply that 1st Workforce directly employs every person featured
* Never present the company as providing services outside its stated recruitment sectors
